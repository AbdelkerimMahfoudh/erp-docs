# 04 — Business Rules

The non-negotiable logic of the phone business. These rules are what keep the data honest and the shop safe. They drive both the backend and the Mistake Prevention System.

---

## A. IMEI & Unit Integrity (the sacred rules)
1. **An IMEI is unique across the entire system.** The same IMEI can never exist twice as "in stock."
2. **An IMEI can be sold exactly once.** Selling an already-sold IMEI is **blocked**, not warned. (A returned unit must first be marked Returned/back-in-stock.)
3. Every IMEI must be **valid**: 15 digits, passes Luhn checksum. Invalid IMEIs are rejected at entry with a clear reason.
4. Dual-SIM devices may carry **two IMEIs**; both are recorded and both are unique.
5. Every IMEI-tracked unit always has exactly one **status**: `In stock · Reserved · Sold · Returned · Faulty · In transit · Transferred out`.
6. Every status change is **logged** with who/when. A unit's full history is always reconstructable.

## B. Stock Rules
7. You cannot sell what you do not have. Selling requires an in-stock unit at the selling branch.
8. Non-serialized items (accessories) are tracked by **quantity**; quantity cannot go negative.
9. **Stock lives at a branch.** Moving stock between branches requires a **transfer**, and stock is "in transit" until the receiving branch confirms.
10. **Cost price is recorded per unit at purchase.** Profit uses the actual cost of the specific unit sold (not an average), so margins are exact.
11. A unit unsold beyond the owner-set threshold (default suggestion: 60 days) is **dead stock** and surfaced for action.
12. "Days of stock left" per product = current quantity ÷ recent daily sales velocity.

## C. Pricing & Margin Rules
13. Every product has a **cost** and a **selling price**; **margin = price − cost**.
14. Selling **below cost** is possible only with an explicit override, is flagged, and is logged.
15. Discounts are bounded by role (employee limit < manager limit < owner unlimited). Exceeding the limit requires a higher-role approval.
16. Prices are never silently overwritten — price history is kept.

## D. Sales & Payment Rules
17. A sale records: unit(s)/product(s), price, cost (for margin), discount, employee, branch, timestamp, payment method(s), customer (optional).
18. **Partial payment / credit** is allowed (0074), including nothing paid at all: the unpaid balance is a **receivable owed by exactly one debtor** — a customer (selected, or typed with a name and optional phone) **or** a partner store with an accepted connection. A sale leaving money owing without a debtor is refused. The receivable is the sale's own `balance_due`; there is no second ledger.
19. A payment in one method may total **less** than the sale, never more (the rest is owed by the named debtor, 18). **A split** (two or more methods, `docs/60` D92, amending `docs/59` D83) takes at most **four places** — the drawer, if used, is one, each account one more — each place once, and pays **all or part** of the sale: what is left is owed by the named debtor like any balance, never more than the total, and amount paid + amount owed = the sale amount; the server refuses a fifth place, a place used twice (`duplicate_payment_destination`), more than the total (`overpayment`) and a balance nobody owes (`debtor_required`) (2026-09-27). Status comes only from money received: **Unpaid** (nothing), **Partially paid**, **Paid in full** (nothing left) — never from a request having succeeded.
19a. **A later payment is not a second sale** (0074). The phone is sold once, on the original sale, with its revenue and profit on that sale's day. Money received later is a payment dated **the day it arrived**: it raises cash (cash only) or the chosen account (that account only), lowers the balance by the same amount, and never raises sales count, sales value or profit. Zero, negative and overpayments are refused, as are inactive accounts, future or pre-sale dates and signed-off days. **A payment in the same displayed minute as its sale is not pre-sale** (`docs/61` §9, 2026-09-28): the phone names a minute and the sale is an instant inside one, so the server records it at the sale's own instant — never before the sale, still in its minute and business day, the chosen minute kept in the audit; an earlier minute is refused. Each collection carries one idempotency key bound to its payload.
19b. A payment keeps the account label and provider **as they were** when the money arrived; renaming or deactivating the account later rewrites no history.
19c. **Finding the phone to sell** (scan, typed IMEI or stock) reads one existing unit and never creates stock. A phone in **another branch** is disclosed only to `branch.manage` (status and branch name); everyone else gets "This phone is not available in this branch.", identical to a number that exists nowhere. The server decides.
19d. **IMEI visibility.** Full: the typing field, the scanner result, the completed Sale detail (authorised staff) and the customer receipt (warranty proof; no cost, margin or internal ids). Masked to the last four: stock lists, the pre-payment preview, Money previews, notifications and compact summaries. Lookup and uniqueness always use the full IMEI.
19e. **The customer's invoice** (2026-09-22) carries only what the server confirmed about that sale: store and branch, invoice number, date and time, the named customer or "cash customer", each item with its exact variant, its full IMEI or serial (19d), quantity, unit price and line total, subtotal, discount, total, every payment by method and by the account label as it stood when the money arrived (a later collection says when), amount paid, remaining, **Paid / Partially paid / Unpaid**, who owes the balance (customer or partner store, with a phone when one is recorded), who served, and the return-policy sentence **of that sale's own snapshot** — never a hardcoded "no returns". Never: cost, margin, profit, any internal id, staff history, permissions, another shop's data, or a claim that a provider verified a payment. One generator, HTML → PDF, page-width for A4 and Letter, every value escaped, EN/FR/AR with correct direction.
20. A **return** reverses the sale: unit goes back to stock (or Faulty), receivable/refund adjusted, and the event is logged. Returns are never a silent delete.
21. Nothing financial is **hard-deleted**. Corrections are reversals/adjustments, preserving the audit trail.

## E. Supplier & Payables Rules
22. A purchase creates stock **and** (if unpaid) a **supplier payable** with a due date.
23. Supplier balances are tracked continuously; overdue payables surface in "Needs Your Attention."
24. Paying a supplier reduces the payable and is logged.

## F. Daily Closing Rules
24a. **The business day begins at 06:00 in the company's local timezone** (0076, `docs/50`). Every money record stores the business date it was assigned when written (`sales`, `payments`, `supplier_payments`: a `business_date` beside the immutable instant; expenses, refunds, settlements, corrections, reversals: their existing date columns, now assigned by the same rule), and every report, rollup, expected-cash figure, per-channel movement, "collected" figure and closing keys on that stored date. Historical rows keep the UTC calendar date they were always reported under; nothing is recalculated. Only the Owner may start the next business date before 06:00; at 06:00 it starts by itself and the previous day, if not closed, reads **Needs review**.
24c. **An expense has two dates, and only one of them counts money** (`docs/57`, `docs/58`). `spent_on` is the day the person *says* the money went out — typed in the form, today by default, a memo kept for continuity with the pre-workflow column; it is shown in the form's review step, filters the API's expense list when asked, and bounds the "unusual amount" sample — and **it moves no figure**. The **accounting day** of a variable expense is its `confirmation_date`, the branch's business date when an Owner confirmed it (rule 24a); of a fixed expense its `due_date`. Home, Money, the Daily closing, the rollups, the results and the goals read only the accounting day. A memo date that disagrees with the accounting day is a note to tidy, never a reason to move money, and is corrected only as an audited Owner act on explicit approval.
25. A close is a **counted snapshot and a history event** (0076, supersedes "closed once"). **While the current business day is closed, the counter waits** (`docs/60` D91 and `docs/61`, 2026-09-27, supersede every automatic reopen): the server refuses a sale, a receipt and **a later payment on a debt** with 409 `store_closed`, naming the closed day — to every client, older app builds included — and writes nothing: no payment, no money movement, no reopen. **Nothing reopens a day by itself**; the Owner or a delegate reopens it deliberately while it is still the current business date. A refused request leaves nothing under its key, so the same key after the opening records the payment once. A reopened day reads **Reopened · review due**, keeps its first snapshot, its actor and its time, and is closed again as a *reclose* whose record distinguishes the movement since the first count from the revised whole-day total; every close stores its whole report, so no later reopen, recount or correction changes an earlier record (0078). Once 06:00 has passed, a closed day is final and is corrected only through the append-only correction workflow. **The phone's counter waits too** (`docs/59` D76): Home disables Sell and Receive and offers **Open store now** — the deliberate reopen, with the before-06:00 choice, for the Owner or a delegate — and the Sell and Receive screens are guarded the same way; a refusal that still reaches a screen covers it with the closed-store panel instead of losing the work in progress; a refused later payment keeps its form, with *Open store now* for those who may and who-can-open for anyone else.
24b. **The Daily closing report is built from records, never re-entered** (0078, `docs/51`). For one business date it shows, from what the checkout, the expenses, the refunds, the purchases and the corrections already recorded: the sales (count, value, approved returns, collected for those sales and still owed on them); the money received and paid out per channel — cash and each configured account — with collections on older debts apart from today's sales; the confirmed expenses (a fixed one on its due date); the result (gross profit = sales − approved returns − the recorded cost of the items sold; the result after the expenses recorded to the date, fixed costs counted in full on their due date; *Cannot calculate yet* when any item sold or returned has no recorded cost — never a zero); and the expected balances (the drawer = the last counted opening + cash in − cash out; an account = the movement staff recorded, never its balance). Every total equals the sum of its lines, or the day cannot be closed. Sales and expenses are shown to `report.view` or `closing.perform`; cost and profit additionally need `cost.view`, and are left out of the response otherwise.
25a. **The boutique's opening is a recorded act, never an inference** (0077, `docs/50` §6). Whoever opens the door records it ("Open the boutique", `closing.count`): an `opened` event with the store-local time and the person, shown as *Opened today · date · time* on the day's card and in its timeline. **The 06:00 cutoff starts a reporting date and never creates an opening event**; a day nobody recorded reads *No opening time recorded*, and its first sale is labelled *First activity*, never *Opened*. Opening a day that was already closed is the reopen of rule 25 (the closing authority) and reads *Reopened today*. Only the current business date can be opened, and not twice while it is open. **Before 06:00 the opening names its business day first** (`docs/56`): the calendar date has moved on but the business date has not, so the Owner is asked — *Continue yesterday's date* selected by default, *Start today's date early* as the explicit alternative, with both dates and the store's time — **before** anything is recorded; anybody else is told which business day the opening, and every sale after it, belongs to. The choice is kept on the opening event and its audit row; an early start writes the same `day_started_early` event a reopen does, then opens the new date; nothing already recorded moves whichever day is chosen, and the day left behind, if never closed, reads *Needs review*.
26. **A physical check is optional** (0078, supersedes Milestone E's "every channel counted or skipped"). A channel counted after any reopen is closed as counted, and any difference is recorded and flagged (possible shortage/theft/error). Every other channel is closed as **not verified** — never as counted, matched or zero — and closing with any channel not verified requires the person closing to **acknowledge it and give a reason**, both kept with the close — **or**, since `docs/58` D71, to **attest that they checked it**: the channel is then closed as **attested** ("Checked; amounts not recorded"), with the person and the time and no amount, no reason asked; it never reads as counted or matched, shows no difference, and a reopen makes it stale like any earlier count. The phone's closing popup offers the two honest ways — enter the amounts, or attest — and nothing else. A close says one of them, never both (`conflicting_statements`); only the close writes the keys these statements leave on a channel (`ATTESTED_AT_CLOSE`, `NOT_VERIFIED_AT_CLOSE`) — a count carrying either as its skip reason is refused (`reserved_reason`), since an attestation stored that way would lift the acknowledgement without anyone attesting. A person's own skip, acknowledged at the close, stays their skip with their reason; attested over at the close, the channel becomes attested, and the skip's reason stays in its count's event and audit. A count taken before a reopen proves nothing about the drawer now and is not reused. On a reclose an undecided difference follows the new figure (or is closed as "no difference remains"); a difference somebody already decided stays decided and any remainder becomes a new one; a channel not checked at that close answers nothing and leaves an earlier question as it was — nothing financial is deleted.
26a. Sale money is counted on the business date it **arrived** (`payments.business_date`, assigned from `paid_at`), in the closing, in per-channel movements and in "collected" — never on the sale's day (0074, 0076). Every movement appears in exactly one day. The Money screen's "cash in the drawer" is the same expected figure the closing uses. **Cash carries forward**: the drawer's opening balance on a business day is the counted cash at the last locked close **whose drawer was actually counted** plus the net cash movement of every day between (0078: a close without a count anchors nothing) — a balance, never income; a shop that has never locked a day starts from zero. Account figures are recorded movement, never a balance: the app does not see the account itself. **Money held** (`docs/60` D89, 0082) is a separate, tracked figure: each method's latest **anchor** plus every movement recorded after it — the drawer anchored only by a locked close whose drawer was counted (the same anchor as the closing), an account by the amount the Owner read in the provider's app (`money_anchors`, append-only, `money.anchor.record`); a method without an anchor is unknown and then no total is shown. It is what the app tracks, never the provider's balance.
27. The app **says when a prior day was never closed** — *Needs review* on Home and on Closing & history — and never blocks a sale for it. A past day on which **nothing at all was recorded** (no sale, payment, count, opening, close, expense, refund, purchase or correction) reads **No activity recorded** instead: it is not an overdue closing (0078). Closing & history opens **any business date** (today, yesterday, or one chosen from the last sixty): its standing, its opening, every count, close, reopen and recount with the store-local time and the person, and — for a day closed before the timeline existed (0076) — its closing row and counts read back as the events they were. Every earlier snapshot stays.
28. **A confirmed record is never edited; it is corrected** (0078, 0079 — `docs/51` §15). Every correction is asked for with a reason by the Owner or a manager (`financial.correction.request`), previewed by the server with nothing written, approved by the Owner (`financial.correction.approve`) — who may ask and approve in one step, the two acts recorded apart — posted to the **current open business day** of the record's branch (it waits while that day is closed), at most one approved per record, and kept for ever in the audit history with its before and after. The record itself, its own day and every earlier close stay exactly as they were. What can be corrected:
    - **a sale that should not exist as recorded** (wrong item, wrong price, a duplicate): cancelled whole — its value, cost and units come off the day it is cancelled (the Daily closing, Results, Home and every goal, branch or personal; the day it was sold keeps it as it was sold and closed — 0080), its phones and accessories go back in stock, the money still held for it leaves the channels it is in (given back or never received), nothing is owed on it any more; then the right sale is recorded again. Refused while it has a return (correct it on the return) or a phone on it has moved since;
    - **a payment** recorded in the wrong channel, all or part (moved), or **never received**, all or part (reversed: the customer or partner store owes it again; a walk-in sale has nobody to owe it, so the sale is cancelled instead);
    - **a confirmed expense** that was wrong, all or part: the money comes back to its channel and the day's expenses fall by it — never before the day the expense counts on;
    - **a stock purchase payment** made from another channel than recorded (moved);
    - **a stock purchase that should not exist as recorded** (goods not received, wrong cost, wrong product): cancelled whole while everything it brought is still in stock at its branch — its phones leave the books (`voided`), its accessories leave stock at the purchase's cost, the money paid comes back; receiving the same phone again brings back the same record, so an IMEI is still one record for ever.
    Not available, by name: reversing a purchase payment as "not paid" (purchases are paid in full and there is no supplier debt to carry it — cancel the purchase instead); editing a sale line or cancelling part of a sale (cancel it and sell again); a sale with a return; a purchase whose goods have moved (correct the later record first). Reassigning a stored business date after the fact is an audited Owner correction that does not exist yet (0076 records the need).
28a. **Closing authority** (0076): the Owner, plus **at most two named delegates per branch** — a Store Manager or a Store Employee, granted and revoked from Team — may count, close, reopen and reclose the day (`closing.perform`). No role other than the Owner's grants it by itself (0078 removed it from two legacy roles); the two named delegates hold it through their delegation, at their own branch only — verified on a real database on 2026-09-25. Seeing the Daily closing report confers nothing, and closing authority carries no correction authority. Counting alone (`closing.count`) stays with every store role. Starting the next day early (`closing.start_early`) is the Owner's alone and is never delegated. Enforced on every mutation and protected read on the server.
28b. After a counted close changes, **the Owner is told**: the reopen, each sale completed while the day is open again, and the reclose — in-app always, by WhatsApp when a number and a channel exist — with the branch, the local time and the business date, a sale's item and value (collected and owed shown apart on a partial payment), never a full IMEI, a cost, a customer's number or another tenant's data. Each notice carries a stable event id, so a retry never repeats it, and a failed delivery never delays or undoes a sale or a close.

## G. Expense & Profit Rules
29. **Net profit = gross margin − expenses** for the period. Margin alone is never presented as profit.
30. Recurring expenses (rent, salaries) auto-generate each period from templates.

## H. Branch & Access Rules
31. Data is scoped by branch; users see only branches they're assigned to (owner sees all).
32. Cost/profit visibility follows role (see roles doc), enforced on the **server**, never just hidden in the UI.

## I. Mistake-Prevention Triggers (plain-language warnings)
The system actively watches for and flags:
- Duplicate IMEI (block).
- **Inventory mismatch** — expected stock vs. counted stock differs (flag for review).
- Sell below cost / abnormally low price / **wrong-cost** (warn + override + log).
- Price off by an order of magnitude (likely a typo — "Did you mean 45,000, not 4,500?").
- Storage/variant mismatch vs. recognized product.
- Selling a Reserved/Faulty/Transferred unit (block or warn per status).
- **Unauthorized actions** — blocked by server-side RBAC and logged.
- Cash reconciliation mismatch at closing.

**35. Discount monitoring is opt-in.** Discount-limit enforcement and "unusual discount / employee pattern" flagging are **OFF by default** and only active when the **Owner enables them** in Settings. Without it enabled, discounts are allowed within normal sale flow; with it enabled, discounts above the configured role limit require approval and deep-discount patterns are surfaced. This keeps small trusting shops simple while giving larger ones control.

**36. WhatsApp sends the short closing summary, and — since 0076 — the three Owner notices of rule 28b** (a day reopened after a counted close, each sale completed while it is open again, the reclose). Amounts appear only when the Owner's WhatsApp setting allows them. **No full IMEI, no cost, no customer phone number and no other tenant's data** ever leave via WhatsApp; full detail stays in the in-app notification center (DailyDigest and the day's history).

## J. Audit & Trust
33. **Every** create/edit/delete/override/status-change is attributable to a user and timestamped.
34. The audit trail is append-only and cannot be erased from within normal app flows.

## Assumptions to confirm with the owner
- **Currency & tax:** single currency assumed; VAT/tax handling is **out of scope for v1** unless required. *(Confirm.)*
- **Costing method:** exact per-unit cost (recommended for IMEI goods) rather than FIFO/average. *(Confirm.)*
- **Dead-stock threshold** default 60 days, **low-stock/days-left** thresholds owner-configurable. *(Confirm defaults.)*
- **Returns window:** is there a max return period? *(Assumption: configurable, default none.)*
- Can a sale be **fully deleted** by an owner, or only reversed? *(Recommendation: reverse only.)*
